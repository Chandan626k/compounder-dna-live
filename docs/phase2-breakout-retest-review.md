# Breakout Lifecycle Trader Review

Post-implementation challenge outcome:

- Swing Trader: lifecycle is understandable and distinguishes candidate, confirmation, pending retest, successful retest, continuation and failure.
- Short-Term Trader: price confirmation is separated from volume confirmation; weak volume does not become a false failure.
- Active Trader: timeframe is explicit; no hidden intraday reinterpretation of daily evidence.
- Risk Specialist: invalidation is required before verified risk/reward; targets are sourced from canonical reaction zones.
- Long-Term Investor: technical lifecycle remains context only and does not alter fundamental authority.
- Data Reliability: pivot confirmation and event timestamps are observation-time based; missing evidence fails closed.
- CTO: implementation remains one canonical engine path; scanner/market-engine do not get a second breakout engine and production BUY/SELL authority remains untouched.

Open semantic boundary: daily is first-class. Intraday lifecycle variants remain future explicit-timeframe work, not implicit reuse of daily bars.
